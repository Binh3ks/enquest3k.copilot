## KEY DIFFERENCES: Week 1-3 vs Week 4+

| Feature | Week 1-3 (Basic) | Week 4+ (Variant) |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Structure** | Free conversation | Objective-based |
| **Question format** | No canonical_question field | question_variants (3 per objective) |
| **Hints** | default_hints at mission level | hints per question variant |
| **Student invitations** | Optional in conversation | 2 per mission (type: "invitation") |
| **Objectives array** | ❌ Not used | ✅ 9-12 objectives |

---

## WEEK 1-3 STRUCTURE DETAILS

### Mission Structure:
- 3 missions per week
- Free-form conversation (AI determines questions based on mission_context)
- conversation_topics guides AI on what to discuss
- example_questions are suggestions, not requirements

### No Objectives Array:
Week 1-3 don't use objectives array. Instead, AI uses:
- mission_context (instructions)
- conversation_topics (topics to cover)
- example_questions (suggested questions)
- minimum/maximum_turns (flexibility)

### Hints:
- default_hints at mission level (3-5 words)
- Not per-question (since questions are dynamic)

## EXAMPLE: Mission Template (Week 1-3)

```javascript
{
  mission_id: 1,
  title: "First Day at School",  // Distinctive title for TurnManager
  title_vi: "Ngày Đầu Tiên Đi Học",
  theme: "Self-introduction",
  
  nova_greeting: "Hello! I am Ms. Nova, your English teacher. What is your name?",
  default_hints: ["My", "name", "is", "I", "am"],
  
  mission_context: `The student is on their first day at school. Ms. Nova is their new English teacher. This is a warm, friendly introduction where the student practices saying "I am [name]" and "I am [age] years old". Keep conversation natural and encouraging.

⚡ FLEXIBLE CONVERSATION:
- If student asks YOU questions → Answer naturally like a human friend
- Allow 12-15 turns if conversation is interactive
- If student goes off-topic → Acknowledge warmly, then redirect
- Be human-like: answer questions fully, don't rush`,
  
  target_vocab: ["name", "age", "student"],
  target_pattern: "I am [name/age/student]",
  
  conversation_topics: [
    "Student's name (What is your name?)",
    "Student's age (How old are you?)",
    "Being a student (Are you a student?)",
    "First day feelings (How do you feel today?)",
    "Simple closing (Great! Nice to meet you!)"
  ],
  
  example_questions: [
    "What is your name?",
    "How old are you?",
    "Are you a student?",
    "How are you today?"
  ],
  
  minimum_turns: 15,
  maximum_turns: 20,
  
  success_criteria: [
    "Uses 'I am' correctly for name",
    "States age with 'years old'",
    "Uses 'student' naturally",
    "Engages naturally in conversation"
  ]
}
```
