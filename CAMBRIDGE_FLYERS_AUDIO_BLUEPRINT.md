## 1. Part 1: Listen and Draw Lines (Scene Investigation)
* **Characters**: Adult Examiner/Teacher (**Woman** or **Man**) and Student (**Girl** or **Boy**).
* **Word Count**: **220 – 280 words**.
* **Structure Invariants**:
  1. **Rubric**: `"Nova: Look at Part 1. Now look at the picture. Listen and look. There is one example."`
  2. **Example Turn**:
     * Student or Adult points to a person and describes their action/clothes.
     * Partner confirms their identity.
     * Rubric Transition: `"Nova: Can you see the line? This is an example. Now you listen and draw lines."`
  3. **5 Test Items**:
     * 5 distinct conversational exchanges (2–4 sentences each).
     * High density of **relative clauses** (`the boy who is slipping...`, `the lady who is wearing...`).
     * **Prepositional phrases of place** (`near the yellow sign`, `in front of the lockers`, `down the hallway`).
     * **Contrasting distractors & clarifications** (*"Is he wearing a green cap? No, he's the one in the blue shirt."*).
  4. **Distractor Name (1 unmatched name)**:
     * One name in the list is NOT in the picture or mentioned with a clear reason why they are absent (*"Is Alex there today? No, Alex is staying at home today."*).

## 2. Part 2: Listen and Write (Form / Notebook Completion)
* **Characters**: Adult interviewer/teacher and student.
* **Word Count**: **180 – 240 words**.
* **Target Elements (5 items + 1 Example)**:
  1. Name spelling letter-by-letter with natural pauses (e.g., `M-I-A`, `W-I-L-S-O-N`).
  2. Number / Quantity / Room Number / Time.
  3. Day / Date / Specific detail.
  4. Duty / Action / Object.
  5. Location / Place.

---

## 3. Part 3: Visual Matching (Match Letters A–H to 5 Items)
* **Characters**: Conversational dialogue between Adult and Student or Storyteller.
* **Word Count**: **200 – 260 words**.
* **Structure**:
  * 5 target items matched against 8 possible picture cards (A to H).
  * Clear backstory and reason for each match, eliminating the 3 distractor locations naturally.

---

## 4. Part 4: Picture Multiple Choice (3 Pictures per Question)
* **Word Count**: **45 – 65 words per question** (~250 – 300 words total).
* **Distractor Rule (Mandatory)**:
  * **ALL THREE visual options (A, B, C) MUST be naturally mentioned in each dialogue turn.**
  * Use past intentions, negative conditions, or alternative suggestions so only the correct option answers the exact prompt.
  * Example: *"Did he fall in the science lab? (Option B) No, he was near the science lab, but he actually fell on the wet floor in the corridor! (Option A) And not on the playground either. (Option C)"*

---

## 5. Part 5: Color and Write (Scene Action Mission)
* **Characters**: Examiner/Nova and Student.
* **Word Count**: **180 – 240 words**.
* **Structure**:
  * 3 color instructions with specific target objects and vibrant colors (Blue, Green, Yellow, Red, Purple).
  * 1 write instruction: spelling out a short, meaningful word in CAPITAL letters on a sign, banner, or label (e.g. `SAFE`, `CARE`, `HELP`).
