# AI TUTOR CONVERSATION GUIDE - WEEK 16: SPORTS COMMENTARY

## WEEK OVERVIEW
- **Topic:** Sports and Physical Activities
- **Grammar Focus:** Present Continuous Tense (am/is/are + verb-ing)
- **Key Vocab:** kick, throw, catch, run, jump, score, hit, pass, cheer, goal
- **STEM Seeds:** energy, motion, team
- **Learning Mode:** Dual-mode (Advanced: detailed, Easy: simplified)

---

## TEACHING STRATEGIES

### 1. VOCABULARY INTRODUCTION
**Approach:** Action-based learning with physical demonstrations

**Dialogue Pattern:**
```
Tutor: "Can you show me how you KICK a ball? 🦵⚽"
Student: [demonstrates or describes]
Tutor: "Great! When you kick, you use your foot to hit the ball. Can you say: 'I kick the ball'?"
Student: repeats
Tutor: "Perfect! Now try: 'I AM KICKING the ball right now!' This means you're doing it NOW."
```

**Focus Words:**
- **Action verbs:** kick, throw, catch, run, jump, hit, pass
- **Sports terms:** score, goal, team, cheer
- **STEM concepts:** energy (power to move), motion (moving), team (working together)

**TPR Activities:**
- Ask student to ACT OUT each verb while saying it
- Use "Simon Says" with Present Continuous: "Simon says: You are jumping!"
- Play "Freeze Dance" - when music stops, describe what they WERE doing: "You were running!"

---

### 2. GRAMMAR FOCUS: PRESENT CONTINUOUS

**Core Pattern:**
```
I am + verb-ing
You/We/They are + verb-ing
He/She/It is + verb-ing
```

**Teaching Sequence:**

**Step 1: Establish "RIGHT NOW" concept**
```
Tutor: "What are you doing RIGHT NOW?"
Student: [answer]
Tutor: "Yes! You ARE TALKING to me. You are LEARNING English. This is happening NOW."
```

**Step 2: Demonstrate with sports actions**
```
Tutor: "Look at this picture. What is the girl doing?"
Student: "She... kick ball?"
Tutor: "Almost! She IS KICKING the ball. Say it with me: She is kicking."
Student: repeats
Tutor: "Great! What about this boy?"
Student: "He is running!"
Tutor: "Perfect! You got it! 🎉"
```

**Step 3: Personal practice**
```
Tutor: "Tell me: What are YOU doing right now?"
Student: "I am... learning?"
Tutor: "Excellent! I AM LEARNING. What else? Tell me about your hands."
Student: "I am... typing?"
Tutor: "Yes! I am typing! What about your eyes?"
Student: "I am reading!"
Tutor: "Perfect! You understand Present Continuous! 🌟"
```

**Common Mistakes to Correct:**
❌ "I kick ball now" → ✅ "I am kicking the ball now"
❌ "She run fast" → ✅ "She is running fast"
❌ "They is playing" → ✅ "They are playing"

### 3. READING & EXPLORE STATION

**Advanced Mode: "The Big Game"**
- 3rd person narrative about Tom's soccer game
- Bold words: 16+ sports vocabulary
- Comprehension focus: sequence of events, character actions

**Easy Mode: "My Soccer Game"**
- 1st person narrative ("I am playing...")
- Simpler sentences, personal experience
- Comprehension focus: basic details, feelings

**Teaching Approach:**
```
Tutor: "Let's read about a soccer game! Have you ever played soccer?"
Student: [shares experience]
Tutor: "Great! As we read, I want you to find all the BOLD WORDS. These are our new vocabulary!"

[After reading]
Tutor: "Who kicked the ball in the story?"
Student: [answers]
Tutor: "Yes! And what happened when Lisa kicked the ball toward the goal?"
Student: "They scored!"
Tutor: "Perfect! They SCORED a GOAL! Can you use 'is scoring' in a sentence?"
Student: [attempts]
Tutor: "Excellent try! How about: 'Lisa is scoring a goal for her team!'"
```

### 4. LOGIC LAB: DUAL-TAB STRUCTURE

#### **Tab 1: Logic & Science (3 questions)**

**Advanced Questions:**
1. Skills matching (Tom throws, Lisa catches - who should be goalkeeper?)
2. Comparison ordering (Anna > Ben > Chen - who jumps highest?)
3. Part-whole subtraction (11 players - 4 defenders - 3 midfielders = ? forwards)

**Easy Questions:**
1. Simple skills match (Tom runs fast, Lisa jumps high - who runs in race?)
2. Number comparison (Anna: 3 balls, Ben: 5 balls - who has more?)
3. Basic subtraction (5 players, need 6 - how many more?)

**Teaching Strategy:**
```
Tutor: "Let's think like detectives! 🕵️ Read the clues carefully."
Tutor: "Tom CAN THROW far but CANNOT CATCH. Lisa CAN CATCH well. Who should be the goalkeeper?"
Student: "Lisa?"
Tutor: "Why Lisa? What does a goalkeeper do?"
Student: "Catch the ball!"
Tutor: "Exactly! Goalkeepers need to CATCH the ball, so Lisa is perfect! Great thinking! 🧠"
```

**Key Phrases:**
- "Let's read the clues step by step"
- "What do we know? What are we looking for?"
- "Can you explain your thinking?"

#### **Tab 2: Singapore Math (5 problems)**

**Advanced Problems:** Larger numbers (5+6=11, 8-5=3, 11-7=4, 3+2=5, 12-9=3)
**Easy Problems:** Smaller numbers (3+2=5, 6-4=2, 8-5=3, 2+3=5, 9-7=2)

**All use BAR MODELS** - visual rectangular diagrams

**Teaching Strategy:**
```
Tutor: "Look at this BAR MODEL. Do you see the two parts?"
Student: "Yes, red and blue."
Tutor: "The red part shows 3 balls, and the blue shows 2 balls. If we put them together, how many do we have?"
Student: "5!"
Tutor: "Perfect! 3 + 2 = 5. The bar helps us SEE the math! Can you draw a bar model for 4 + 3?"
```

**Bar Model Types:**
- **Part-Whole:** Split bar showing parts that make a whole
- **Comparison:** Two bars side-by-side showing difference

### 5. MINDMAP STATION

**Structure:** 6 stems × 6 branches = 36 combinations

**Stems (Advanced & Easy same):**
1. I kick... (the ball, it far, it hard, to my friend, in soccer, to score)
2. I run... (very fast, in the park, with my team, to get the ball, every day, and jump)
3. I throw... (the ball, to you, high up, with energy, in basketball, and catch)
4. I catch... (the ball, with my hands, quickly, and pass, in baseball, it well)
5. I jump... (very high, in the air, for joy, with energy, to catch, and run)
6. My team... (is winning, scored a goal, plays together, is happy, has energy, is the best)

**Teaching Approach:**
```
Tutor: "Let's build sentences like LEGO! Each stem is the beginning, and you pick a branch to finish it."
Tutor: "Start with 'I kick...' Now choose: the ball / it far / it hard. Which one?"
Student: "The ball."
Tutor: "Great! 'I kick the ball.' Now try with 'I run...'"
Student: "I run very fast!"
Tutor: "Perfect! Can you make it Present Continuous?"
Student: "I AM RUNNING very fast!"
Tutor: "Excellent! You're mastering this! 🌟"
```

### 6. WRITING STATION

**Prompt (Easy):** "Write about your favorite sport. What do you do? Who do you play with?"
**Prompt (Advanced):** "Describe a sports game you played or watched. Use Present Continuous tense."

**Scaffolding Questions:**
```
Tutor: "Let's plan your writing! First: What sport did you choose?"
Student: [answers]
Tutor: "Good! Now, what ACTIONS do you do in this sport? Use our vocabulary words!"
Student: [lists actions]
Tutor: "Excellent! Now, can you put one action into Present Continuous?"
Student: "I am kicking the ball."
Tutor: "Perfect! That's your first sentence. What happens next?"
```

**Writing Checklist (guide student):**
- ✅ Named the sport
- ✅ Used at least 3 vocabulary words (kick, run, jump, etc.)
- ✅ Used Present Continuous (am/is/are + verb-ing)
- ✅ Said who you play with (team, friends, family)
- ✅ Explained why you like it

### 7. ASK AI STATION

**Sample Prompts:**
1. "I want to play soccer. What should I do?"
2. "How can I run faster?"
3. "What sports are good for kids?"
4. "Why do we need to play in a team?"
5. "What should I eat before playing sports?"

**Tutor's Role: Guide Critical Thinking**
```
Tutor: "Great question! Before I answer, what do YOU think? Why might teamwork be important?"
Student: [shares idea]
Tutor: "That's a smart thought! Let me add to it. When you play in a TEAM, you can PASS the ball to each other. One person can't win alone! Everyone uses their ENERGY and works together. Does that make sense?"
Student: "Yes!"
Tutor: "Can you think of a time when teamwork helped you?"
Student: [shares]
Tutor: "Perfect example! That's exactly what teamwork means in sports too!"
```

### 8. WORD POWER STATION

**Collocations:**
1. **kick the ball** - "Players kick the ball to score goals."
2. **score a goal** - "Mia scored a goal! Her team is winning!"
3. **run fast** - "Athletes run fast to win the race."

**Teaching Pattern:**
```
Tutor: "These words LOVE to be together! We don't say 'kick A ball' - we say 'kick THE ball'. It's like best friends!"
Tutor: "Can you use 'score a goal' in a sentence with Present Continuous?"
Student: "She is scoring a goal!"
Tutor: "Perfect! Now YOU try making a sentence about running fast."
Student: "I am running fast!"
Tutor: "Excellent! These phrases will make your English sound natural! 🎯"
```

## ADAPTIVE TEACHING TIPS

**If student struggles with grammar:**
- Simplify to just "I am + verb-ing" first
- Use lots of visuals and gestures
- Practice with their current actions (typing, sitting, reading)

**If student excels:**
- Challenge with negative forms: "I am NOT running, I am walking."
- Ask questions: "What is he doing?" "What are they playing?"
- Encourage storytelling: "Describe what's happening in this sports video"

**For shy students:**
- Start with yes/no questions: "Is he kicking the ball?"
- Use chat typing instead of speaking at first
- Praise every attempt enthusiastically

**For active learners:**
- Incorporate movement: stand up and act out verbs
- Use screen sharing to play sports video clips
- Let them draw or show objects while describing actions
