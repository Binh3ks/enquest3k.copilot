#### W16+ ADVANCED MODE: Scientific method integration
**Mission Example: "Design a Plant Experiment"**
```javascript
{
  mission_id: 1,
  story_character: {
    name: "Professor Green",
    role: "Research Botanist",
    personality: "Analytical, challenges students, uses academic language",
    speaking_style: "Uses scientific terms, asks for reasoning"
  },
  opening_narrative: `
    Professor Green's lab is full of plants under different lights.
    "We're testing a hypothesis today," she says. "Can plants photosynthesize without sunlight?"
  `,
  mission_context: `
    STUDENT TASK: Design and conduct a controlled experiment
    STEM VOCABULARY: hypothesis, variable, control group, photosynthesis, chlorophyll, observation, conclusion
    SCIENCE CONCEPT: Scientific method (Question → Hypothesis → Experiment → Observation → Conclusion)
  `,
  objectives: [
    {
      stepKey: "hypothesis",
      question_variants: [
        "What do you predict will happen to a plant kept in complete darkness for a week?",
        "State your hypothesis using 'If...then' format."
      ],
      hints: ["If sunlight is removed, then..."],
      correct_path: "If a plant has no sunlight, then it cannot photosynthesize and will lose its green color"
    },
    {
      stepKey: "design_experiment",
      question_variants: [
        "We need two groups. Which variables should we keep the same?",
        "What is the only difference between the control group and experimental group?"
      ],
      hints: ["Same water, same soil, same temperature. ONLY sunlight is different."],
      correct_path: "Control group: normal sunlight. Experimental group: no sunlight. All other variables identical."
    },
    {
      stepKey: "observation",
      question_variants: [
        "After one week, what did you observe? Describe the color change.",
        "Why do you think this happened? Explain using the word 'chlorophyll'."
      ],
      correct_path: "The plant in darkness turned yellow because chlorophyll needs sunlight to function"
    }
  ]
}
```
**Advanced features:**
- ✅ Academic vocabulary (hypothesis, variable, control, photosynthesis)
- ✅ Structured scientific thinking (state hypothesis, design experiment, interpret results)
- ✅ Cause-effect argumentation ("because", "if...then")

## 📌 3. PHÂN BIỆT EASY vs ADVANCED MODE TRONG STEM (W16+)

| **Criteria** | **EASY MODE** | **ADVANCED MODE** |
|---|---|---|
| **Vocabulary** | Everyday science terms<br>(plant, animal, water, magnet, metal, hot, cold) | Academic terms<br>(photosynthesis, precipitation, hypothesis, variable, ecosystem, chlorophyll) |
| **Context Complexity** | Concrete, observable phenomena<br>("Ice melts when it's hot") | Abstract concepts<br>("States of matter change due to energy transfer") |
| **Reasoning** | 1-step logic<br>("Plants need water → Water helps plants grow") | Multi-step reasoning<br>("No water → No photosynthesis → Chlorophyll breaks down → Plant dies") |
| **Question Types** | YES/NO<br>Simple multiple choice<br>"What do plants need?" | Explain WHY<br>Predict outcomes<br>"What happens if you remove sunlight from the system?" |
| **Visual Support** | Heavy visual support<br>(diagrams, photos, bar models for every problem) | Text-heavy, minimal visuals<br>(Only key diagrams, expect text reasoning) |
| **Math Difficulty** | Basic arithmetic<br>(5 + 3 = 8, 12 - 7 = 5) | Pre-algebra<br>(? + 5 = 12, work backwards)<br>(2 pens = 5 pencils price, solve for pencil) |
| **Science Depth** | Fun facts<br>("Giraffes have long necks"<br>"Magnets stick to metal") | Cause-effect + Function<br>("Long necks evolved as an adaptation to reach high leaves"<br>"Magnetism is caused by aligned electron spins") |
| **Grammar Complexity** | Simple sentences, present simple<br>("The caterpillar eats leaves") | Complex sentences, conditionals, passive<br>("If the caterpillar doesn't eat enough, it won't have energy to form a cocoon") |

**Critical Rule:** Advanced ≠ Easy + longer text  
- Advanced must differ in **depth of reasoning**, not just length
- Advanced should require **inference**, not just recall
- Advanced uses **academic vocabulary**, not just more words

**TRƯỚC KHI BẮT ĐẦU:**
1. ✅ **Xác định Station**: Đang tạo nội dung cho station nào? (Read, AI Tutor, Logic Lab, Grammar, etc.)
2. ✅ **Xác định Week Theme**: Chủ đề tuần là gì? (e.g., W16 "Time Traveler", W20 "Under the Sea")
3. ✅ **Chọn STEM Topic**: Chủ đề khoa học/toán nào phù hợp?
   - Tham khảo PHASE progression (W16-54 = STEM Intro, W55-120 = Applied, W121+ = Advanced)
   - Đảm bảo align với Week theme (e.g., "Under the Sea" → Marine biology, Water pressure)

**RESEARCH & ACCURACY:**
4. ✅ **Research Chuẩn**: Tìm kiếm thông tin từ nguồn đáng tin:
   - SGK Khoa học VN (lớp tương ứng)
   - Singapore Science/Math Textbooks
   - Khan Academy Kids (English science content)
   - National Geographic Kids
5. ✅ **Fact-Check**: Đảm bảo kiến thức khoa học CHÍNH XÁC
   - NO misconceptions (e.g., ❌ "Heavy objects fall faster" - SAI)
   - Use correct scientific terminology
   - Age-appropriate complexity (no quantum physics for 6-year-olds)

**CREATE CONTENT:**
6. ✅ **Viết EASY MODE First**:
   - Simple vocabulary (everyday science terms)
   - Concrete, observable context (experiments kids can see/do)
   - Heavy visual support (diagrams, photos, bar models)
   - 1-step reasoning (direct cause-effect)
   - Grammar level = Week syllabus standard
   
7. ✅ **Viết ADVANCED MODE**:
   - Academic vocabulary (scientific terms in English)
   - Abstract reasoning (inference, multi-step logic)
   - Minimal visual scaffolding (text-heavy)
   - Complex grammar (conditionals, passive voice, relative clauses)
   - Verify: Advanced ≠ Easy + more words. Must differ in DEPTH.

8. ✅ **Test Both Modes**:
   - Easy: Can a 6-year-old ESL beginner understand with pictures?
   - Advanced: Does this challenge a 10-12 year old aiming for competitive exams?

**VALIDATION:**
9. ✅ **Science Accuracy Review**: 
   - Have a science teacher or subject expert review
   - Cross-check facts with 2+ reliable sources
   
10. ✅ **Language Quality Check**:
    - Is the English natural and native-like?
    - Are there typos or grammar errors?
    - Does it match the reading level target?

11. ✅ **Dual-Mode Differentiation Check**:
    - Easy and Advanced are DIFFERENT in meaningful ways
    - Not just "Easy = 5 sentences, Advanced = 10 sentences"
    - Different vocabulary tiers, reasoning depth, scaffolding levels

### C. Red Flags (CẤM KỴ khi tạo STEM content):

**SCIENCE ACCURACY:**
- ❌ **Sai sự thật khoa học**
  - "The sun revolves around the Earth" ❌
  - "Heavy objects fall faster than light objects" ❌ (Galileo proved this wrong)
  - "Humans only use 10% of their brain" ❌ (Myth)

**AGE-APPROPRIATENESS:**
- ❌ **Quá khó cho độ tuổi**
  - Phase 1 (W16-54, age 6-9): NO quantum mechanics, calculus, advanced chemistry
  - Use grade-level benchmarks: Singapore/VN Grade 1-3 for Phase 1+, Grade 4-6 for Phase 2, Grade 6-8 for Phase 3

**CONTEXT & CLARITY:**
- ❌ **Thiếu context**
  - DON'T: "Photosynthesis is important." (What is it? Why does it matter?)
  - DO: "Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air. This process is called photosynthesis."

**DUAL-MODE DIFFERENTIATION:**
- ❌ **Advanced = Easy + longer text**
  - DON'T: Easy = "The plant needs water" (5 words); Advanced = "The plant requires water" (4 words but uses "requires") ← NOT ENOUGH DIFFERENCE
  - DO: 
    - Easy: "Plants need water to grow" (concrete, simple)
    - Advanced: "Without water, photosynthesis cannot occur because water is one of the essential reactants" (abstract, scientific reasoning)

**LANGUAGE QUALITY:**
- ❌ **Unnatural English**
  - "The butterfly it is beautiful" (grammar error)
  - "Magnet can to stick metal" (verb form error)
  - Always have native English speaker review OR use verified SGK English science textbooks
