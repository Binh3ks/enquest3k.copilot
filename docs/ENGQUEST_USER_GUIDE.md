# 📚 ENGQUEST USER GUIDE
## Smart English Learning App for Students Age 6-10

---

## 🎯 INTRODUCTION

**Engquest** is an interactive English learning app specially designed for Vietnamese elementary students. The app helps children:

✅ **Pre-learn content** that will be taught in class  
✅ **Review knowledge** in a fun and engaging way  
✅ **Practice 4 skills**: Listening - Speaking - Reading - Writing  
✅ **Reach B1+ level (IELTS 5.0-5.5)** after 3 years  

**Special Feature**: AI Tutor (Ms. Nova) - an intelligent virtual teacher available 24/7 to chat and help students!

---

## 🚀 GETTING STARTED

### Step 1: Login
- Open Engquest app on computer or mobile
- Enter your name (e.g., "Anna", "Tom")
- Select the week corresponding to your class (Week 1, Week 2...)

### Step 2: Choose Learning Mode
The app has **2 modes**:

| 🟢 **EASY MODE** (Basic) | 🔴 **ADVANCED MODE** (Advanced) |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Simple vocabulary | Academic vocabulary |
| Short, easy sentences | Longer, complex sentences |
| Suitable for beginners | Suitable for advanced learners |

**Tip**: Start with **Easy Mode**, then try **Advanced Mode** after completing all activities to challenge yourself!

### Step 3: Start Learning
- Choose one of the 15 Learning Stations below
- Follow instructions in each activity
- Earn stars ⭐ when completed

---

## 📖 15 LEARNING STATIONS

### 🎨 **GROUP 1: VOCABULARY & READING**

#### 1️⃣ **Read & Explore**
**What to do**: Read a short story or article about the weekly topic

**How to use**:
- 🔊 Press **Play** to hear the audio
- 👆 Tap **bold words** to see meanings and images
- 🇻🇳 Press **"Translate sentence"** for Vietnamese translation

**Tips**: 
- Read aloud with the model voice to practice pronunciation
- Re-read the passage 2-3 times for better understanding

---

#### 2️⃣ **New Words** (10 Core Vocabulary)
**What to do**: Learn the 10 most important words of the week

**3 exercises**:
1. **Listen & Type**: Hear the word and type it correctly
2. **Meaning Match**: Match English words with Vietnamese meanings
3. **Fill in Context**: Fill in blanks in sentences

**Tips**:
- Study 3-4 words daily instead of all 10 at once
- Write new words in a notebook for review

---

#### 3️⃣ **Word Power**
**What to do**: Learn additional vocabulary (3-7 words/week)

**Word types**:
- **Phase 1** (Weeks 1-54): Collocations (ride a bike, take a bath)
- **Phase 2** (Weeks 55-120): Synonyms/Antonyms (big = huge, small ≠ large)
- **Phase 3** (Weeks 121+): Idioms (figure out, point out)

**Tips**: These words help you speak English more like a native speaker!

---

#### 4️⃣ **Daily Watch**
**What to do**: Watch English videos from YouTube (3-5 minutes)

**Video types**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Educational cartoons (Peppa Pig, English Singsing)
- **Weeks 55-120**: Science for kids (SciShow Kids, Nat Geo Kids)
- **Weeks 121+**: TED Talks, News, Debates

**Tasks after watching**:
- Answer 3-5 questions about the video
- Write 2-3 sentences about what you learned

**Tip**: Turn on English subtitles (not Vietnamese) to practice reading!

---

### 🧠 **GROUP 2: SKILLS PRACTICE**

#### 5️⃣ **Ask AI**
**What to do**: Practice asking questions in English

**3 levels**:
1. **Shadow Asking** (Repeat):
   - Example: "Ask AI: What do lions eat?"
   - Press 🎤 mic and repeat the sentence

2. **Guided Asking** (With hints):
   - Given keywords: "Why / volcanoes / erupt"
   - You form: "Why do volcanoes erupt?"

3. **Free Inquiry** (Free):
   - AI states an opinion, you question it
   - Example: AI says "Homework is good" → You ask about drawbacks

**Tips**: 
- Use **Mic** instead of typing to practice speaking
- Don't be afraid of mistakes - AI will correct and help you!

---

#### 6️⃣ **Grammar**
**What to do**: Complete 20 grammar exercises about the weekly topic

**Exercise types**:
- Multiple choice
- Word ordering
- Verb form filling

**Tip**: Do 5-10 questions daily instead of all 20 at once

---

#### 7️⃣ **Dictation**
**What to do**: Listen to sentences and type them or arrange words

**2 modes**:
- **Weeks 1-120**: Short sentences (5-10 words)
- **Weeks 121+**: Passages, note main ideas

**Tips**: Listen 2-3 times before you start writing!

---

#### 8️⃣ **Shadowing** (Pronunciation Practice)
**What to do**: Read along with the model voice to practice pronunciation and intonation

**3 steps**:
1. **Listen**: Hear the standard voice, see karaoke-style text
2. **Record**: Press 🎤 and read
3. **Feedback**: AI scores and highlights mispronounced words

**Tips**:
- Easy Mode has "Slow" button for slower playback
- Read loudly and clearly
- Repeat until you score 80+

---

#### 9️⃣ **Writing Challenge**
**What to do**: Write a short paragraph about the weekly topic

**3 submission methods**:
1. **Speech-to-Text**: Speak your ideas, app converts to text
2. **Photo Submission**: Write on paper, take a photo
3. **Type**: Type directly into the input box

**Requirements**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Minimum 40 words
- **Weeks 55-120**: Minimum 100 words
- **Weeks 121+**: Minimum 150 words

**Tips**:
- Use newly learned vocabulary
- Word Bank below provides suggestions
- Proofread before submitting

---

#### 🔟 **Mindmap Speaking**
**What to do**: Look at a mind map and speak about the topic

**Example**:
```
Topic: MY FAMILY
     👨‍👩‍👧 Family
    /     |     \
Father   Mother  Sister
(Doctor) (Teacher) (Student)
```
→ Speak: "I have a small family. My father is a doctor..."

**Tip**: You don't need to be perfect - just express your ideas!

---

### 🔬 **GROUP 3: KNOWLEDGE EXPLORATION**

#### 1️⃣1️⃣ **Explore**
**What to do**: Read science/social studies articles related to the weekly topic

**Topics**:
- Science (How plants grow, How rain forms)
- Social Studies (Community helpers, Different cultures)
- History (Ancient Egypt, Dinosaurs)

**Tip**: This is harder than Read & Explore, but helps you learn content and English simultaneously!

---

#### 1️⃣2️⃣ **Logic Lab**
**What to do**: Solve math and logic problems in English

**3 levels**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Match shapes with words, color patterns, read equations
  - Example: "One plus one equals two"
  
- **Weeks 55-120**: Word problems
  - Example: "Johnny has 5 apples. He gives 2 to his friend. How many apples does he have?"
  
- **Weeks 121+**: Read charts, statistics
  - Example: "Look at the bar chart. Which month has the most rain?"

**Tip**: This is Math English - very useful for other subjects!

---

### 🎮 **GROUP 4: LEARNING GAMES**

#### 1️⃣3️⃣ **Word Match**
**What to do**: Play matching games with words/images

**How to play**:
- Flip cards to find pairs: English word + image
- Or: English word + Vietnamese meaning
- Faster = more points!

---

#### 1️⃣4️⃣ **Game Hub**
**What to do**: Play 5 vocabulary learning games

**Games**:
1. **Crossword**: Fill words into grid based on clues
2. **Word Search**: Find hidden words in letter grid
3. **Multiple Choice**: Choose correct answers quickly
4. **Fill in Blanks**: Fill words into blanks
5. **20 Questions**: Guess objects by asking Yes/No questions

**Tip**: Play 10-15 minutes daily to learn while having fun!

## 💡 EFFECTIVE LEARNING TIPS

### 1. **Daily Schedule:**

| Day | Suggested Activities (30-40 minutes) |
|-----|--------------------------------------|
| **Monday** | Read & Explore + New Words (1-5) |
| **Tuesday** | New Words (6-10) + Grammar (10 questions) |
| **Wednesday** | Ask AI + Daily Watch + Word Power |
| **Thursday** | Grammar (10 questions) + Dictation + Shadowing |
| **Friday** | Writing Challenge + Logic Lab |
| **Saturday** | Game Hub + AI Tutor (Story Mission) |
| **Sunday** | AI Tutor (Free Talk + Quiz) + Weekly review |

### 2. **3-3-3 Rule:**
- Study **3 times/day**, each **30 minutes**
- Or: Study **3 minutes** whenever free (on bus, break time...)
- Review after **3 days, 3 weeks, 3 months**

### 3. **Prioritize Speaking:**
- Always use 🎤 **Mic** instead of typing when possible
- Speak loudly and clearly
- Don't fear mistakes - AI will help you correct!

### 4. **Learn in Order:**
Follow this sequence:
1. Read & Explore (understand topic)
2. New Words (learn vocabulary)
3. Other stations (practice)
4. AI Tutor (comprehensive review)

### 5. **Use Technology:**
- **Listen more**: Play Daily Watch during breakfast
- **Speak more**: Talk with Ms. Nova daily
- **Write regularly**: Keep an English diary
- **Play games**: 10-15 minutes/day for fun learning

## 🔧 AUDIO SETTINGS (TTS Settings)

You can customize voice and speed:

### Choose Voice:
- 🌟 **Ms. Nova** (Warm): Friendly female voice, suitable for children
- 🌙 **Ms. Luna** (Calm): Gentle female voice, easy listening
- ⭐ **Ms. Stella** (Energetic): Lively, cheerful female voice
- 🎵 **Mr. Orion** (Professional): Polite male voice

### Choose Speed:
- **Auto**: Automatic (speed depends on activity)
- **0.7x**: Very slow (for beginners)
- **0.85x**: Slow (for pronunciation practice)
- **1.0x**: Normal (standard speed)
- **1.2x**: Fast (for advanced learners)

**Tips**: 
- Beginners should use **0.85x** or **0.7x**
- After getting used to it, switch to **1.0x** for natural listening

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

### ❓ Which week should I start from?
**Answer**: Start from Week 1 if you're a beginner. If you've studied English before, take the Quiz in AI Tutor to determine your level.

### ❓ How many minutes should I study daily?
**Answer**: Minimum **30 minutes/day**. Ideally **40-60 minutes** split into 2-3 sessions.

### ❓ Can't hear audio?
**Answer**: 
1. Check speakers/headphones
2. Check volume settings
3. Try changing voice in TTS Settings
4. Reload the page

### ❓ Microphone not working?
**Answer**:
1. Allow browser to access Mic (when prompted)
2. Check if Mic is properly connected
3. Test Mic on other apps (Zoom, Skype...)

### ❓ How do I know I've completed a Station?
**Answer**: When finished, you'll see:
- Green ✅ checkmark
- Receive ⭐ star
- Update on Dashboard

### ❓ Should I do Easy or Advanced Mode?
**Answer**:
- First time learning a new week: **Easy Mode**
- After understanding well: **Advanced Mode**
- Goal: Complete both modes!

### ❓ Ms. Nova doesn't answer me?
**Answer**:
1. Check Internet connection
2. Wait 5-10 seconds (AI needs time to think)
3. Try asking a simpler question
4. Reload page if error persists

### ❓ Can I revisit old weeks?
**Answer**: Yes! You can select any week to review. Stars are retained.

### ❓ Can I learn offline?
**Answer**: Currently the app needs Internet to:
- Load new content
- Use AI Tutor (Ms. Nova)
- Auto-grade assignments

Some parts like Game Hub can be played offline after loading.
