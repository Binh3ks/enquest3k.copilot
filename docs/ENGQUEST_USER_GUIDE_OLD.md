# 📚 ENGQUEST USER GUIDE
## Smart English Learning App for Students Age 6-10

---

## 🎯 INTRODUCTION

**Engquest** is an interactive English learning app specially designed for Vietnamese elementary students. The app helps children:

✅ **Pre-learn content** that will be taught in class  
✅ **Review knowledge** in fun and engaging ways  
✅ **Practice 4 skills**: Listening - Speaking - Reading - Writing  
✅ **Reach B1+ level (IELTS 5.0-5.5)** after 3 years  

**Special Feature**: AI Tutor (Ms. Nova) - an intelligent AI teacher available 24/7 to chat and help students!

---

## 🚀 GETTING STARTED

### Step 1: Login
- Open Engquest app in your web browser
- Create account or login as Guest to try
- Enter your name and choose avatar

### Step 2: Choose Learning Mode
The app has **2 modes** (can switch anytime):

| 🟢 **EASY MODE** (Basic) | 🔴 **ADVANCED MODE** (Advanced) |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Simple, familiar vocabulary | Academic vocabulary |
| Short sentences (5-8 words) | Longer, complex sentences (8-14 words) |
| Suitable for beginners | Suitable for advanced learners |
| Focus on personal topics (family, school) | Focus on global topics (science, society) |

**How to switch**: Click **"EASY MODE"** or **"ADVANCED"** button on left sidebar

**Tips**: 
- First time learning new week → Start with **Easy Mode**
- After completing Easy Mode → Try **Advanced Mode** for challenge
- Ultimate goal: Complete **both modes**!

### Step 3: Start Learning
- Choose week (Week 1, Week 2...) from left sidebar
- Select one of 15 learning stations
- Follow instructions in each activity
- Earn % progress when completed

## 📖 15 LEARNING STATIONS

### 📚 **GROUP 1: READING & VOCABULARY**

#### 1️⃣ **Read & Explore**
**How it works**:
- Read a short story or passage (6-12 sentences) about weekly topic
- 10 key vocabulary words are **bolded** in text
- Auto-play audio with native pronunciation

**How to use**:
- 🔊 Click **Play** button to hear audio
- 👆 **Tap bolded words** → popup shows:
  - Image illustration
  - Vietnamese definition
  - 🔊 button to hear pronunciation
- 🇻🇳 Click **"Translate"** for sentence-by-sentence translation

**Study tips**:
- Listen at least 2 times before reading
- Read aloud following model voice
- Try to understand before checking translation
- Write new words in notebook

---

#### 2️⃣ **New Words** (10 Core Vocabulary)
**How it works**:
- Learn 10 most important words of the week
- Each word has: image, audio, definition, example sentence
- 3 drill exercises to memorize

**3 exercises**:
1. **Listen & Type**: Hear word → Type correct spelling
2. **Meaning Match**: Drag English word to Vietnamese meaning
3. **Context Practice**: Fill word in sentence blank

**Study tips**:
- Study 3-4 words per day instead of all 10 at once
- Write new words in notebook with drawings
- Create new sentences with learned words

---

#### 3️⃣ **Word Power**
**How it works**:
- Learn extended vocabulary (collocations, phrases, idioms)
- Each word has: definition, example, audio
- 4 drills per word: Copy 3x, Write definition, Write sentence, Flip card

**Word types by phase**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Collocations (ride a bike, take a bath)
- **Weeks 55-120**: Synonyms/Antonyms (big = huge)
- **Weeks 121+**: Idioms & Phrasal Verbs (figure out)

---

#### 4️⃣ **Daily Watch**
**How it works**:
- Watch English YouTube videos (3-5 minutes)
- Videos carefully curated for weekly topic
- Answer questions after watching

**Video types**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Educational cartoons (Peppa Pig, Numberblocks)
- **Weeks 55-120**: Science for kids (SciShow Kids, Nat Geo Kids)
- **Weeks 121+**: TED Talks, News, Debates

---

### 🗣️ **GROUP 2: COMMUNICATION SKILLS**

#### 5️⃣ **Ask AI**
**How it works**:
- Practice asking questions in English
- 5-7 prompts (situations) per week
- Each prompt has hints

**3 scaffolding levels**:
1. **Shadow Asking** (Weeks 1-20): Repeat complete question
2. **Guided Asking** (Weeks 21-80): Form sentence from keywords
3. **Free Inquiry** (Weeks 81+): Free debate questions

**How to use**:
1. Read prompt
2. Click 🎤 **Mic** and speak (recommended)
3. Or type in input box
4. Click **Check** for feedback

---

#### 6️⃣ **Mindmap Speaking**
**How it works**:
- Look at mindmap with keywords
- Click 🎤 and speak about topic
- AI scores based on length, vocabulary, grammar

---

### 📝 **GROUP 3: WRITING & GRAMMAR**

#### 7️⃣ **Grammar**
**How it works**:
- 20 grammar exercises per week
- Focus on 1-2 specific grammar points
- Auto-grading

**Exercise types**:
- Multiple Choice
- Word Order (arrange words)
- Fill in Blanks

---

#### 8️⃣ **Writing/Video Challenge**
**How it works**:
- Write paragraph about weekly topic
- Or record presentation video
- Word bank suggestions below

**3 submission methods**:
1. **Speech-to-Text**: Speak → App converts to text
2. **Photo Submission**: Write on paper → Take photo
3. **Type Directly**: Type in input box

**Length requirements**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Minimum 40 words
- **Weeks 55-120**: Minimum 100 words
- **Weeks 121+**: Minimum 150 words

---

### 👂 **GROUP 4: LISTENING & PRONUNCIATION**

#### 9️⃣ **Dictation**
**How it works**:
- Listen to English sentences and write
- 2 modes: Typing or Word Order
- Unlimited replays

**2 types**:
- **Weeks 1-120**: Short sentences (5-10 words)
- **Weeks 121+**: Passages, note-taking

---

#### 🔟 **Shadowing** (Pronunciation Practice)
**How it works**:
- Read along with model voice (karaoke style)
- Record your voice
- AI scores and highlights mispronounced words

**3 steps**:
1. **Listen**: Hear standard voice, see karaoke text
2. **Record**: Click 🎤 and read
3. **Feedback**: Receive score /100, see mistakes

**Tips**: Aim for 80+ score before moving on

---

### 🔬 **GROUP 5: KNOWLEDGE EXPLORATION**

**Topics**: Science, Social Studies, History, Geography

---

#### 1️⃣2️⃣ **Logic Lab**

**3 levels**:
- **Weeks 1-54**: Math vocabulary (circle, plus, equals)
- **Weeks 55-120**: Word problems

### 🎮 **GROUP 6: LEARNING GAMES**

**2 modes**:

#### 1️⃣4️⃣ **Game Hub**
**How it works**:
- 3 interactive English learning games
- Each game focuses on specific skill
- Plus World Adventure - 4 external game websites

**3 Internal Games**:

**1. Show & Tell Ladder** 🪜:

**2. Make Sentence** ✍️:
- Create complete sentence from keywords

**3. Ask Me** ❓:
- AI gives a word
- You ask questions about it
- Example: Word = "pencil" → Ask: "What color is your pencil?"
- Practice Question Formation

**World Adventure** (4 external sites):
- 🎮 **English Arcade** - Mixed games
- 🌍 **Word Games Park** - Vocabulary games
- 🧩 **Quiz Battle** - Quiz games
- 🧸 **ESL Games Plus** - Various ESL games

## 🤖 **AI TUTOR - MS. NOVA**

**Ms. Nova** is a smart, friendly AI teacher with American accent, available 24/7!

### How to open AI Tutor:
- Click **✨ "Ms. Nova"** button at bottom-right corner (purple floating button)
- Large chat window appears on right side
- Can open from any page (global widget)

### 4 Tabs in AI Tutor:

#### 1️⃣ **Story Mission**
**How it works**:
- Chat with Ms. Nova following mission storyline
- 3 missions per week
- Must complete 12-15 conversation turns

**How to play**:
1. Choose mission from menu (e.g., "Help Oliver find his backpack")
2. Ms. Nova tells story and asks you questions
3. You answer by:
   - Typing in chat
   - Or clicking 🎤 **Mic** and speaking (recommended!)
4. Ms. Nova responds and continues story
5. Try to use target vocabulary to earn points

**Mission structure**:
- **Opening**: Ms. Nova introduces situation
- **Body** (12-15 turns): Back-and-forth conversation
- **Conclusion**: Ms. Nova summarizes and praises

**Scoring based on**:
- Sentence length (5+ words better than 1-2 words)
- Using target vocabulary
- Grammar correctness (Ms. Nova gently corrects)

**Tips**:
- Write long sentences (5-10 words) instead of short "Yes/No"
- Use target vocabulary for Ms. Nova's praise
- If stuck, read hints below
- Don't hesitate to replay missions for better score

---

#### 2️⃣ **Free Talk**
**How it works**:
- Completely free conversation with Ms. Nova
- No fixed script or missions
- Ms. Nova responds like a friendly assistant

**Topics you can discuss**:
- Hobbies: "I like playing soccer"
- Family: "Tell me about my family"
- School: "What did you do at school today?"
- Ask Ms. Nova for knowledge: "What animals live in jungle?"

**Tips**:
- Don't hesitate to ask anything in English
- Ms. Nova corrects mistakes gently and naturally
- Practice 5-10 minutes daily
- Treat Ms. Nova as a friendly companion!

---

#### 3️⃣ **Pronunciation**
**How it works**:
- Ms. Nova teaches word-by-word pronunciation
- Word list from weekly New Words
- Record and receive instant score

**How to learn**:
1. Choose word from list (week's 10 words)
2. Listen to Ms. Nova read
3. Click 🎤 **Record** and read
4. Receive score /100 and feedback
5. If < 80 score → read again

**3 practice modes**:
- **Word only**: Practice single word
- **With sentence**: Word in context
- **Repeat 3x**: Train reflexes

**Tips**:
- Practice each word 3-5 times until 90+ score
- Pay attention to stress: **PEN**cil not pen**CIL**
- Listen carefully to ending sounds: desk /k/
- Use headphones for better listening

---

#### 4️⃣ **Debate** 🔒
**🔓 Unlocks: Week 20**

**How it works**:
- Discuss and express opinions with Ms. Nova
- Practice critical thinking
- Learn to argue in English

**How to play**:
1. Ms. Nova presents debate topic
   - Example: "Should students wear uniforms?"
2. You choose: 👍 Agree or 👎 Disagree
3. Explain reasons in 3-5 English sentences
4. Ms. Nova argues back
5. You continue defending your viewpoint

**Tips**:
- Use connectors: Because, However, But, So
- Give specific examples: "For example..."
- Don't need 100% correct opinion - important to speak!
- Best way to practice advanced Speaking!

## 🎯 PROGRESS SYSTEM

### How progress works:
- Each completed Station = **% progress increases**
- Each week has **15 stations** (not counting Review Dashboard)
- Complete all 15 stations = **100%** for that week

### Track progress:
**1. Week Progress**:
- % bar on sidebar (left)
- % badge on corner of each week
- ✅ Green badge when 100%

**2. Station Progress**:
- Each station has individual indicator
- Color-coded: 
  - 🟢 Green = Completed
  - 🟡 Yellow = In Progress
  - ⚪ Gray = Not Started

**3. Review Dashboard**:
- Overview of all weeks
- SRS (Spaced Repetition System) review
- Must complete Review to unlock next week (from week 6)

### Continue Learning:
- Purple card at top of sidebar
- Click → return to last station you were studying
- Shows current week % progress

## 🔧 AUDIO SETTINGS (TTS Settings)

You can customize voice and speed in **AI Tutor tabs and some stations**:

### Settings location:
- **AI Tutor tabs**: Top-right corner of header (next to progress)
- **Stations**: Some stations have individual settings

### 4 Voices:
- 🌟 **Ms. Nova** (Warm): Friendly female, suitable for children ⭐ Default
- 🌙 **Ms. Luna** (Calm): Gentle female, easy listening
- ⭐ **Ms. Stella** (Energetic): Lively, cheerful female
- 🎵 **Mr. Orion** (Professional): Polite male

### 5 Speeds:
- **Auto**: Automatic (Pronunciation: 0.85x, Conversation: 1.0x, Reading: 0.95x)
- **0.7x**: Very slow (for complete beginners)
- **0.85x**: Slow (for pronunciation, shadowing)
- **1.0x**: Normal (standard, natural speed)
- **1.2x**: Fast (for advanced, high-speed listening)

**Settings auto-save**: Your choice is remembered for next time!

## 💡 EFFECTIVE LEARNING TIPS

### 1. **Daily Schedule (30-45 minutes):**

| Day | Activity (30-40 min) | Goal |
|-----|----------------------|------|
| **Mon** | Read & Explore + New Words (1-5) | Familiarize with weekly topic |
| **Tue** | New Words (6-10) + Grammar (10 Q) | Learn vocab + grammar |
| **Wed** | Ask AI + Daily Watch + Word Power | Practice asking + watch video |
| **Thu** | Grammar (10 Q) + Dictation + Shadowing | Listening + pronunciation |
| **Fri** | Writing Challenge + Logic Lab | Writing + math |
| **Sat** | Game Hub + AI Tutor (Story Mission) | Games + conversation |
| **Sun** | AI Tutor (Free Talk) + Review incomplete stations | Weekly review |

### 2. **3-3-3 Rule:**
- Study **3 times/day**, each **30 minutes**
- Or: Study **3 minutes** whenever free
- Review after **3 days, 3 weeks, 3 months** (SRS)

### 3. **Speaking First:**
- ✅ Always use 🎤 **Mic** instead of typing when possible
- ✅ Speak loudly, clearly, confidently
- ✅ Read aloud Read & Explore daily
- ❌ Don't fear mistakes - mistakes are for learning!

### 4. **Learn in sequence:**
1. **Read & Explore** → Understand topic
2. **New Words + Word Power** → Learn vocabulary
3. **Grammar** → Understand structures
4. **Practice stations** → Apply knowledge
5. **AI Tutor** → Review and natural communication

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### ❓ Which week should I start from?
**→** Always start from **Week 1**, even if you know English. Content is designed sequentially.

### ❓ How many minutes daily?
**→** Minimum **30 minutes/day**. Ideally **40-60 minutes** split into 2-3 sessions.

### ❓ Can't hear audio?
**→**
1. Check speakers/headphones
2. Check browser and computer volume
3. Try changing voice in TTS Settings
4. Reload page: Cmd+R (Mac) or Ctrl+R (Windows)
5. Try different browser (Chrome recommended)

### ❓ Microphone not working?
**→**
1. **Allow browser to access Mic**: Click "Allow" when prompted
2. Check Mic properly connected
3. Test Mic on other apps (Zoom, Google Meet)
4. Try different browser

### ❓ How to know station is completed?
**→** When finished, you'll see:
- % increases on sidebar
- Green color on progress indicator
- % badge appears on week corner
- Auto-save indicator shows "Saved"

### ❓ Easy or Advanced Mode?
**→**
- **First time new week**: Always **Easy Mode**
- **After understanding**: Try **Advanced Mode**
- **Ultimate goal**: Complete **both modes**!

### ❓ Ms. Nova doesn't answer?
**→**
1. Check Internet connection
2. Wait 5-10 seconds (AI needs time)
3. Try simpler question
4. Reload page if error persists

### ❓ Can I revisit old weeks?
**→** Yes! You can:
- Select any week from sidebar to review
- % progress is retained
- Redo stations to improve score
- From week 6+: Must complete previous week's SRS Review to unlock next week

### ❓ Can I learn offline?
**→** **No, needs Internet** for:
- Loading lesson content
- Using AI Tutor (Ms. Nova)
- Auto-grading
- Saving progress to server

### ❓ Why is there no Quiz in AI Tutor?
**→** Quiz tab is currently **under development** (coming soon). It will be added in a future version, focusing on common student mistakes to reinforce knowledge gaps.

### ❓ How has Game Hub changed?
**→** Game Hub has been **completely redesigned**:
- **Before**: 5 simple mini-games (Crossword, Word Search...)
- **Now**: 3 deep interactive games + World Adventure (4 external sites)
- Focus on **production** (speaking, writing, question formation)
