## 🔬 PHÂN TÍCH CHI TIẾT TỪNG CÂU HỎI

### **🧬 SCIENCE (30% - Cao nhất!)**

#### **Question 1: Classification (Biology)**
> "Which animals lay eggs? A. turtles, ducks, crabs B. baboons, deer, pigs..."

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Classification (phân loại động vật)
- ✅ Biology knowledge (oviparous vs viviparous)
- ✅ Multiple examples recognition

**So với Logic Lab hiện tại:**
- ❌ Blueprint chưa có: Animal classification questions
- ❌ Chỉ có "T-Rex vegetarian?" (yes/no) - quá đơn giản
- ✅ CÓ: Classification type nhưng chưa đủ sâu

---

#### **Question 2: Physics - Gravity**
> "If four identical balls dropped at same time and height, which lands first?"
> (Hint: Ball 1, 2, 3 normal; Ball 4 có vẻ khác material/density)

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Physics principles (gravity, mass, air resistance)
- ✅ Controlled experiment thinking
- ✅ Variable identification (what's different?)

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ❌ Blueprint HOÀN TOÀN THIẾU: Physics concepts
- ❌ Không có câu hỏi về gravity, force, motion
- 🔥 **GAP NGHIÊM TRỌNG**: Science = chỉ có "plants need sunlight?"

---

#### **Question 3: Photosynthesis**
> "Green plants are important because they _____"
> Answer: produce food and give off oxygen

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Biology process understanding
- ✅ Cause-effect (plants → oxygen → animals survive)
- ✅ Scientific vocabulary (produce, consume, carbon dioxide)

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ✅ CÓ: "Do plants need sunlight?" (Q3 trong blueprint)
- ❌ NHƯNG: Quá shallow (yes/no), không yêu cầu EXPLAIN WHY
- 🔥 **THIẾU**: Multi-choice với các distractor logic (consume vs produce)

---

#### **Question 4: Life Cycles**
> "Mosquito's life cycle: Egg → Larva → Pupa → Adult"

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Sequential thinking (order matters!)
- ✅ Biology knowledge (metamorphosis)
- ✅ Pattern recognition (life cycle stages)

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ❌ Blueprint KHÔNG CÓ: Life cycle questions
- ✅ CÓ: Pattern/Sequence type (Past-Present-Future)
- 🔥 **THIẾU**: Applied sequences (science context, not just abstract)

---

#### **Question 5: Temperature + Ice**
> "Boiling water + 5 ice cubes → temperature after 10 minutes?"
> Options: Nhiều mức nhiệt độ khác nhau

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Physics (heat transfer, melting point)
- ✅ Estimation (không cần công thức, cần REASONING)
- ✅ Scientific method (predict outcome of experiment)

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ❌ Blueprint HOÀN TOÀN THIẾU
- ❌ Không có câu hỏi về temperature, states of matter, heat

---

#### **Question 6: Food Web/Chain**
> "If foxes and hawks removed → what happens to rabbits/snakes/insects?"

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Ecology (predator-prey relationships)
- ✅ System thinking (domino effect)
- ✅ Cause-effect chain (remove predator → prey increases)

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ❌ Blueprint HOÀN TOÀN THIẾU: Ecosystem concepts
- ✅ CÓ: "T-Rex eats meat → vegetarian?" (deductive)
- 🔥 **GAP**: Từ simple deduction → complex system thinking

### **🔢 MATH & LOGIC (20%)**

#### **Question 14: Clock Problem**
> "4 clocks: one correct, one +25 min, one -15 min, one broken. Find correct time."

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Logic reasoning (elimination)
- ✅ Math operations (+/- time)
- ✅ Problem constraint understanding
- ✅ Multi-variable analysis (4 clocks, 3 conditions)

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ✅ Singapore Math CÓ: Before/After timeline (Q5)
- ❌ NHƯNG: Không có logic puzzle với multiple variables
- 🔥 **GAP**: Từ simple subtraction → complex constraint satisfaction

---

#### **Question 15: Venn Diagram (3 circles)**
> "3 circles create 7 areas. Given 3, 5, 7 in 3 areas. Fill 6, 8, 9, 10 in remaining 4 so each circle sum equals. Find sum."

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ **ALGEBRA** (2 pens = 5 pencils → equation)
- ✅ Set theory visualization (Venn diagram)
- ✅ Pattern finding (equal sums)
- ✅ Trial-and-error + logic

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ❌ Blueprint HOÀN TOÀN THIẾU: Venn diagrams
- ❌ Không có bài toán "equal sums" constraint
- 🔥 **CRITICAL GAP**: Đây là dạng toán CHUYÊN, không phải P1/P2

---

#### **Question 16: Algebraic Word Problem**
> "2 pens = 5 pencils price. 3 pens + 10 pencils = 112,000. Find pencil price."

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ **ALGEBRA setup** (2p = 5c, 3p + 10c = 112000)
- ✅ Substitution method
- ✅ Multi-step reasoning

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ✅ Singapore Math CÓ: Simple word problems (eggs, swords)
- ❌ NHƯNG: Chỉ Phase 1 (addition/subtraction)
- 🔥 **GAP NGHIÊM TRỌNG**: 
  - Blueprint P1: 5+3=8
  - Trần Đại Nghĩa: 2p=5c, solve for p/c
  - **Chênh lệch tư duy: 4-5 năm học!**

---

#### **Question 17: Composite Area Calculation**
> "Square ABCD = 8cm, squares MNDQ & DGEF = 6cm. Find darkened area."

**Kỹ năng yêu cầu:**
- ✅ Geometry (area of squares, overlapping regions)
- ✅ Spatial reasoning (visualize overlap)
- ✅ Subtraction/Addition of areas
- ✅ Multi-step calculation

**So với Logic Lab:**
- ✅ Singapore Math P2 CÓ: "Rectangle 8×5 area = 40cm²"
- ❌ NHƯNG: Single shape only
- 🔥 **GAP**: Từ simple area → composite shapes with overlap
