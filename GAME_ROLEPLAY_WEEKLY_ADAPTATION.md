# GAME & ROLEPLAY ADAPTATION - WEEKLY CONTENT MAP

**Objective**: Mỗi tuần có games và roleplay scenarios riêng biệt, gắn chặt với vocabulary và theme của tuần đó.

---

## 🎮 GAME ADAPTATION SYSTEM

### Core Principle
Games sẽ **tự động adapt** dựa trên:
- Target vocabulary của tuần
- Grammar patterns của tuần  
- Theme/context của tuần

### 4 Core Games (Reusable with Weekly Content)

#### 1. 🔗 WORD CHAIN (Nối từ)
**Mechanic**: Say word → Next word starts with last letter  
**Adaptation**: Uses vocabulary from current week

**Week-by-Week Examples**:

```javascript
// WEEK 1: School & Friends
Nova: "Let's play Word Chain with school words! I'll start: TEACHER"
Student: "RULER"
Nova: "Great! RULER ends with R... READING!"
Target Vocab: teacher, desk, book, friend, pencil, chair, board, student, ruler, bag

// WEEK 2: Family
Nova: "Word Chain with family words! I'll start: MOTHER"
Student: "RELATIVE" 
Nova: "Nice! How about... FATHER"
Target Vocab: mother, father, sister, brother, grandma, grandpa, baby, family, cousin, aunt

// WEEK 3: Toys & Games
Nova: "Let's chain toy words! I start: DOLL"
Student: "LEGO"
Nova: "Perfect! LEGO ends with O... hmm... ORIGAMI!"
Target Vocab: doll, ball, car, puzzle, robot, kite, blocks, yo-yo, teddy, skateboard

// WEEK 4: Feelings
Nova: "Feelings Word Chain! I say: HAPPY"
Student: "YELL" (frustrated)
Nova: "Good try! Y is hard... how about YOUNG?"
Target Vocab: happy, sad, angry, scared, excited, tired, proud, shy, calm, surprised

// WEEK 5: House & Rooms
Nova: "House Word Chain! I start: BEDROOM"
Student: "MIRROR"
Nova: "Excellent! MIRROR ends with R... ROOF!"
Target Vocab: bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, living room, door, window, lamp, sofa, table, chair
```

---

#### 2. 🎵 RHYME TIME (Vần điệu)
**Mechanic**: Find words that rhyme  
**Adaptation**: Focus on phonics patterns in weekly vocabulary

**Week-by-Week Examples**:

```javascript
// WEEK 1: School & Friends
Nova: "What rhymes with BOOK?"
Student: "LOOK!"
Nova: "Yes! BOOK, LOOK, COOK! What rhymes with PEN?"
Target Rhymes: book/look, pen/hen, friend/end, desk/chest, class/grass

// WEEK 2: Family
Nova: "Find a rhyme for DAD!"
Student: "SAD!"
Nova: "Perfect! DAD, SAD, MAD, GLAD! Now... BROTHER?"
Target Rhymes: dad/sad, mother/another, baby/maybe, sister/mister

// WEEK 3: Toys & Games  
Nova: "Rhyme with BALL?"
Student: "DOLL!"
Nova: "Great! BALL, DOLL, CALL, TALL! What about CAR?"
Target Rhymes: ball/doll, car/star, kite/light, blocks/socks

// WEEK 4: Feelings
Nova: "What rhymes with HAPPY?"
Student: "SNAPPY!"
Nova: "Nice! HAPPY, SNAPPY, CLAPPY! Now try... SAD?"
Target Rhymes: happy/snappy, sad/mad, scared/shared, calm/palm

// WEEK 5: House & Rooms
Nova: "Rhyme with DOOR?"
Student: "FLOOR!"
Nova: "Excellent! DOOR, FLOOR, MORE, STORE! What rhymes with BED?"
Target Rhymes: door/floor, bed/red, lamp/camp, chair/hair
```

#### 3. ❓ 20 QUESTIONS (Đoán vật)
**Mechanic**: AI thinks of object, student asks yes/no questions  
**Adaptation**: Object is from weekly vocabulary

**Week-by-Week Examples**:

```javascript
// WEEK 1: School & Friends
Nova: "I'm thinking of something in a classroom. Ask yes/no questions!"
Student: "Is it big?"
Nova: "No, it's small!"
Student: "Do you write with it?"
Nova: "Yes! It's a PENCIL! ✏️"
Target Objects: pencil, book, desk, bag, ruler, eraser, chair, board

// WEEK 2: Family
Nova: "I'm thinking of a family member. Guess who!"
Student: "Is it a girl?"
Nova: "Sometimes! Keep asking!"
Student: "Do they read stories?"
Nova: "Yes! It's GRANDMA! 👵"
Target Objects: mother, father, sister, brother, grandma, grandpa, baby

// WEEK 3: Toys & Games
Nova: "I'm thinking of a toy. Can you guess?"
Student: "Can you throw it?"
Nova: "Yes!"
Student: "Is it round?"
Nova: "Yes! It's a BALL! ⚽"
Target Objects: ball, doll, car, puzzle, robot, kite, blocks

// WEEK 4: Feelings
Nova: "I'm thinking of a feeling. Ask me!"
Student: "Is it good?"
Nova: "Not really..."
Student: "Do people cry?"
Nova: "Sometimes! It's SAD! 😢"
Target Objects: happy, sad, angry, scared, excited, tired

// WEEK 5: House & Rooms
Nova: "I'm thinking of something in a house. Guess!"
Student: "Is it in bedroom?"
Nova: "It can be!"
Student: "Do you sleep on it?"
Nova: "Yes! It's a BED! 🛏️"
Target Objects: bed, sofa, lamp, table, chair, mirror, rug, door
```

#### 4. 🧩 SENTENCE BUILDER (Xây câu)
**Mechanic**: Build sentence word-by-word together  
**Adaptation**: Practice weekly grammar patterns

**Week-by-Week Examples**:

```javascript
// WEEK 1: School & Friends (I have... / I like...)
Nova: "Let's build a sentence! I start: I"
Student: "have"
Nova: "Good! I have..."
Student: "a"
Nova: "I have a..."
Student: "friend!"
Nova: "Perfect sentence! 'I have a friend!' ⭐"
Target Patterns: I have a..., I like..., This is my...

// WEEK 2: Family (He/She is... / My ... is...)
Nova: "Your turn! Start with 'My'"
Student: "My"
Nova: "My..."
Student: "sister"
Nova: "My sister..."
Student: "is happy!"
Nova: "Beautiful! 'My sister is happy!' 💕"
Target Patterns: My ... is..., He/She is..., I love my...

// WEEK 3: Toys & Games (I play with... / Can I...?)
Nova: "Let's say: I"
Student: "I play"
Nova: "I play..."
Student: "with my ball!"
Nova: "Awesome! 'I play with my ball!' 🎾"
Target Patterns: I play with..., Can I have...?, I like to play...

// WEEK 4: Feelings (I feel... / I am...)
Nova: "Start: I"
Student: "I feel"
Nova: "I feel..."
Student: "happy today!"
Nova: "Wonderful! 'I feel happy today!' 😊"
Target Patterns: I feel..., I am..., When I..., I feel...

// WEEK 5: House & Rooms (It is in... / There is...)
Nova: "Begin with: There"
Student: "There is"
Nova: "There is..."
Student: "a lamp"
Nova: "There is a lamp..."
Student: "in my bedroom!"
Nova: "Perfect! 'There is a lamp in my bedroom!' 💡"
Target Patterns: There is... in..., It is in..., I ... in the...
```

## 🎭 DYNAMIC ROLEPLAY SYSTEM

### Core Principle
Mỗi tuần có **3-4 roleplay scenarios** gắn với theme, sử dụng vocabulary và context của tuần.

---

### WEEK 1: SCHOOL & FRIENDS 🎒

#### Roleplay 1: 📚 NEW CLASSMATE
```
Character: New Student (bạn mới)
Setting: Classroom
Vocab Focus: desk, pencil, book, friend, teacher

Nova: "Hi! I'm new here. What's your name?"
Student: "I'm [name]. Welcome!"
Nova: "Thank you! Can you show me my desk?"
Student: "Sure! It's here. This is your pencil and book."
Nova: "Oh great! Can we be friends?"
```

#### Roleplay 2: 📖 LIBRARY HELPER  
```
Character: Librarian (thủ thư)
Setting: School Library
Vocab Focus: book, read, story, shelf, quiet

Nova: "Hello! Welcome to the library. Can I help you?"
Student: "I want a book!"
Nova: "What kind of story do you like?"
Student: "I like animal stories!"
Nova: "Perfect! Here's a good book. Enjoy reading!"
```

#### Roleplay 3: ⚽ PLAYGROUND FRIEND
```
Character: Playground Buddy
Setting: School Playground  
Vocab Focus: play, friend, ball, game, fun

Nova: "Hi! Do you want to play with me?"
Student: "Yes! What game?"
Nova: "Let's play catch! Do you have a ball?"
Student: "Yes! Here it is!"
Nova: "Great! You throw first!"
```

## 🎯 HOW IT WORKS

### When Student Selects a Game:

1. **FreeTalkTab detects week number** (from URL: `/week/5/...`)
2. **Loads week content**: `getGameContentForWeek(5)` returns Week 5 house vocabulary
3. **Game prompt includes**:
   - Theme: "House & Rooms"
   - Vocab: bedroom, kitchen, lamp, sofa, table...
   - Game-specific rules with weekly content
4. **AI uses this context** to play the game with relevant vocabulary

### When Student Selects a Roleplay:

1. **FreeTalkTab detects week number**
2. **Loads roleplay scenarios**: `getRoleplaysForWeek(5)` returns 3 house-themed roleplays
3. **Student picks**: Interior Designer / House Tour / Furniture Shop
4. **AI receives**:
   - Character role: "Interior Designer"
   - Vocab focus: bedroom, lamp, sofa, table, chair
   - Opening line: "I design rooms! What room do you want to make beautiful?"
5. **AI stays in character** and uses weekly vocabulary naturally
